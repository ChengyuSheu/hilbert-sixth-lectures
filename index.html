Hilbert's Sixth Problem

An unofficial teaching companion to Deng, Hani & Ma, Hilbert's sixth problem: derivation of fluid equations via Boltzmann's kinetic theory (arXiv:2503.01800; accepted, Annals of Mathematics, Nov 2025), and its companion arXiv:2408.07818. The methodology is taken apart into one lecture and one visualization per idea, each traced to the exact section it expounds — see MAPPING.md.
