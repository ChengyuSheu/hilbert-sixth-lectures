What the picture says

Arrow 1 is valid for $\alpha A t_{\mathrm{fin}}$ below the bound shown. Arrow 2 needs the Boltzmann description to survive $\alpha t\to\infty$ — hydrodynamics is the regime of many collisions per particle. Under Lanford's theorem the first bound is an absolute constant, so the two requirements never meet. Deng–Hani–Ma replace the constant by $(\log|\log\varepsilon|)^{1/2}$, which diverges, and the gap closes.
