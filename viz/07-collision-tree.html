Why the series stops converging

Iterating Duhamel $n$ times produces $s(s+1)\cdots(s+n-1)\sim n!$ collision trees, each carrying the time-simplex factor $t^{n}/n!$ and a power $\alpha^{n}$. The factorials cancel and what is left is a geometric series in $C\alpha t$ — convergent only for $\alpha t$ below an absolute constant. Any longer time needs most terms to be much smaller than the crude bound, which is a statement about their cycle structure: see Lecture 09.
