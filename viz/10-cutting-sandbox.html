Click an atom to cut it free

Cutting partitions $\mathbb{M}$ into $\mathbb{M}_1$ (cut free, integrated first) and $\mathbb{M}_2$ (cut fixed), so that $I_{\mathbb{M}}=I_{\mathbb{M}_1}\circ I_{\mathbb{M}_2}$. Crossing bonds become free or fixed ends — which is why degree changes as you cut. Degree is a property of the molecule together with the cutting sequence.
