The identification that makes it all work

Recollisions = independent cycles. A collision history with no recollisions is a tree; every time two particles that already share history meet again, an edge closes a loop. So $\rho = E - V + c$, the circuit rank, counts recollisions — and the analytic question "how small is this term" becomes the graph-theoretic question "how much can be extracted from a graph of circuit rank $\rho$". Build a history and watch $\rho$ increment.
