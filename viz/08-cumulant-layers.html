Propagating $\lVert E_H(\ell\tau)\rVert_{L^1}\le\varepsilon^{\alpha_*\lvert H\rvert}$ across layers

Induction state

Two ideas, one picture

Propagate structure, not size. A norm bound loses a factor $C>1$ per layer and dies after $O(1)$ layers — Lanford again. The cumulant bound spends that loss on the exponent, which has $\lvert\log\varepsilon\rvert$ of room; the constraint becomes $C^{L}\lesssim\lvert\log\varepsilon\rvert$, i.e. $L\lesssim\log\lvert\log\varepsilon\rvert$.

Partial time expansion. Expand only the cumulants — they come multiplied by a small factor — and leave the $O(1)$ main density $f_A$ frozen at the layer's start. Toggle "expand $f_A$ too" and watch the term counter detonate.

Caricature warning. The per-layer degradation law $\alpha_*\mapsto\alpha_*/C$ is invented for exposition. The real loss in [LT] is inseparable from the combinatorial estimates. What is faithful here is the shape of the conclusion. See Lecture 08 §7.
