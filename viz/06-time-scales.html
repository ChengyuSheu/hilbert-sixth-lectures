Layer degradation (schematic)

Caricature. Each layer is modelled as degrading the cumulant exponent by a fixed factor $C$, so the ansatz $\varepsilon^{\alpha_*\lvert H\rvert}$ stops being informative once $C^{L}\gtrsim\lvert\log\varepsilon\rvert$, i.e. $L\lesssim\log\lvert\log\varepsilon\rvert$. The shape of the conclusion is faithful; the per-layer law is invented for exposition. See Lecture 08 §7.
