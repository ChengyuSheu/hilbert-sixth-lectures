What is being tested

The excess (Definition 3.6) is “the best power of $\varepsilon$ by which the trivial estimate can be improved”. It is additive under cutting, which turns a product bound into a score. Since the number of molecules grows factorially and each independent cycle is one recollision, the requirement is a constant gain per cycle: $\mathrm{excess}(\mathbb{M})\gtrsim c\,\rho(\mathbb{M})$, uniformly. That is Proposition 3.8, and it is the mathematical heart of the paper.

Switch off the long-bond gain and the torus-generated molecules fall below the line. Switch it on and the cloud lifts. That difference is precisely what [H6] adds to [LT].

This illustrates the logic of Proposition 3.8; it does not test it. The scoring is the caricature of Lecture 10 §7 and the molecules are random graphs, not collision histories that a hard-sphere flow can actually realise.
