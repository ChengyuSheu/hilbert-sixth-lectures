Collision sphere: drag $v$ and $v_1$

As $\omega$ sweeps $\mathbb{S}^{1}$, the outgoing pair $(v',v_1')$ traces the circle of centre $\tfrac12(v+v_1)$ and radius $\tfrac12|v-v_1|$ — the locus forced by conservation of momentum and energy. Shading gives the kernel $((v-v_1)\cdot\omega)_+$: zero on the receding half.
