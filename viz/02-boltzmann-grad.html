Why $N\varepsilon^{d-1}$

A sphere of diameter $\varepsilon$ moving at speed $|v|$ for time $t$ sweeps a tube of volume $\asymp\varepsilon^{d-1}|v|t$. With $N$ centres at unit density it meets $\asymp N\varepsilon^{d-1}|v|t$ of them. Holding $N\varepsilon^{d-1}=\alpha$ fixed is the unique way to keep the collision rate finite and non-zero — and it forces the volume fraction $N\varepsilon^{d}\approx\alpha\varepsilon\to0$.
