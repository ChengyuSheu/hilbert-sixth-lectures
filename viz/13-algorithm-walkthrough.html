The amortization framing is this repository's gloss, not the paper's language. [H6] §1.4.2 says: isolate one bad $\{3A\}$-molecule with a long bond per iteration, cut at the long bond, and defer the new obligation to the next iteration. Modelling that as a potential function which must stay non-negative is a helpful way to see why deferral works — see Lecture 13 §7.
