Relaxation onto the Maxwellian manifold

Collisions project the distribution onto the $(d+2)$-parameter Maxwellian manifold; transport then makes it slide along it. That sliding motion is the fluid equation. As $\delta\to0$ (i.e. $\alpha=\delta^{-1}\to\infty$) the projection becomes instantaneous relative to the sliding — that separation of scales is the hydrodynamic limit.
