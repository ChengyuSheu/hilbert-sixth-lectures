Hard sphere dynamics on $\mathbb{T}^2$

The law being applied

$\omega=(x_i-x_j)/\varepsilon\in\mathbb{S}^{d-1}$, and at contact $v_i(t^+)=v_i(t^-)-((v_i-v_j)\cdot\omega)\,\omega$, $\;v_j(t^+)=v_j(t^-)+((v_i-v_j)\cdot\omega)\,\omega$. The normal components are exchanged; the tangential components are untouched. Contacts are located by solving the contact quadratic exactly, so the law is applied at the true contact time.
