1D compressible Euler — characteristics and shock formation

Expansion residual by order

Each order divides the residual by $\delta$. [H6] carries the expansion to $F_6$ because the truncation error must survive being fed through the error terms of Theorem 1. Every $F_n$ involves derivatives of the fluid fields — which is exactly why the whole construction dies at a shock.

Theorem 3

$\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot(\rho u)=0$;   $\partial_t(\rho u)+\nabla\cdot(\rho u\otimes u)+\nabla p=0$;   $\partial_t[\rho\frac{dT+|u|^2}{2}]+\nabla\cdot[\rho u\frac{dT+|u|^2}{2}]+\nabla\cdot(pu)=0$;   $p=\rho T$.

The ideal gas law is derived: for a local Maxwellian, $\int(v-u)\otimes(v-u)\,\mathfrak{M}\,dv=\rho T\,\mathrm{Id}$ automatically. Hard spheres are monatomic, so the specific heat is $d/2$ and $\gamma=(d+2)/d$ — in $d=3$, exactly $5/3$.
